Today (Patients)

The Today page is the central dashboard of Clinical Copilot. It opens by default and gives clinical staff a same-day view of the selected PCP's schedule, along with AI-generated insights and quality metrics.

  1. At the top, Clinical Copilot greets the user and shows the date and number of patients scheduled for today. Below the greeting, an AI-generated insight highlights what needs attention — for example, open care gaps that can be closed during today's visits, patients with recent ER admissions, or pending medication refills. Ask Copilot opens the AI Assistant with this context already in view.

  2. Today's Summary gives an at-a-glance count of:

    • Clarifications — open documentation clarification tasks.
    • Critical Patient(s) — patients flagged as high-priority.
    • Care Gaps — open care gaps across today's patients.
    • HEDIS — open HEDIS tasks across today's patients.

  3. Below the summary, Clinical Copilot lists today's appointments in chronological order, with patient details and visit context. A patient flagged as high-priority shows a Critical label. An appointment row opens its details.

  4. The HEDIS Composite card shows how many HEDIS measures are compliant out of the total tracked for the panel, with the number of measures still needed to reach 100%.

At the bottom of the page, the Ask anything bar sends a question directly to the AI Assistant. Suggested prompts provide one-click starting points for common questions.

Tracking a PCP Group's Performance

Location: Patients > Today

When a PCP Group is selected instead of a single PCP, Today switches to an aggregated Dashboard view for the whole group. Instead of a single day's schedule, the page shows group performance metrics.

Summary row — four scores for the selected group:

  • Overall Score — a composite score across key performance metrics.
  • Members — the total number of unique members managed by the group.
  • Care Gaps Score — how well the group manages and closes care gaps.
  • Utilization — how well the group coordinates care and avoids unnecessary utilization.

Care Gaps Outlook shows the monthly share of addressed care gaps over the last 12 months.

Care Gaps Execution summarizes action and confirmation rates for the group, with targets or period-over-period change where applicable.

Operational Signals includes Membership Pulse, HEDIS Composite, and Utilization Follow-up cards for a compact view of panel health.
