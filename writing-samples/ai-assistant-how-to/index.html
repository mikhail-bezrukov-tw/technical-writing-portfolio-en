How to Use the AI Assistant in Workspace

AI Assistant is embedded in Workspace. On every Workspace page, it can use the context of the patient, provider, or other record you're viewing, so you can ask questions in plain language.

Open AI Assistant

The AI Assistant button is the round button at the right edge of the page.

To open the chat, do one of the following:

  • Click the AI Assistant button.
  • Press Ctrl+Shift+O (⌘+Shift+O on Mac) to open AI Assistant and start a new chat.

The AI Assistant chat opens as a compact panel over the current Workspace page.

Tip

If the button covers something you need, drag it to another place along the left or right edge of the screen. Workspace remembers where you leave it.

If You Don't See AI Assistant

The AI Assistant button appears only for accounts with access to the feature. If you don't see the button on any Workspace page, contact your workspace administrator to request access.

Ask About Workspace Data

Ask AI Assistant about any patient, provider, facility, or other Workspace data from any page, even if you don't have that record open.

If you already have a patient, provider, or other record open, you don't need to name it in your question. AI Assistant uses what you have open as context, no matter which tab is open. It can also answer questions about something you open over the page, such as a checklist, calendar, task, or document.

When you open another page while the chat is open, the conversation continues with the data of the new page.

Some answers appear as cards instead of plain text, for example, a patient list, lab results, care gaps, or appointments.

Tip

Not sure what to ask? Start with “Which of Dr. Smith's patients were admitted to a hospital last month?” or “Summarize Mary Johnson's activity over the last 12 months”, then change the names, dates, or details to fit your work.

Work with the Chat

The AI Assistant chat in Workspace works the same way as in the standalone AI Assistant app.

Note

Chats you start in Workspace are saved to your AI Assistant chat history.

The controls are described below; the numbers match the numbers in the mockup.

#ControlWhat It Does
1New chatStarts a new chat for the page that is open
2Chat listOpens your saved chats. Here you can search, pin, archive, share, or move a chat to a project
3ExpandSwitches the chat between the compact panel and full screen
4CloseCloses the chat panel without deleting the conversation. To return to it, click the AI Assistant button
5Paper clipAttaches a file to your question
6Connected servicesLists the services whose data AI Assistant can use. The number next to the label shows how many are selected. Workspace selects the relevant ones for each page automatically
7Model menuSelects the AI model that answers

Under each answer, you can copy it, regenerate it, or have it read aloud.

Use Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl+Shift+O works on any Workspace page. The other shortcuts work while the chat is open.

Shortcut (Windows)Shortcut (Mac)Action
Ctrl+Shift+O⌘+Shift+OStart a new chat, even if the chat is hidden
Shift+EscShift+EscMove the cursor to the message field
Ctrl+Enter⌘+EnterSend the message
Ctrl+Shift+X⌘+Shift+XStop the answer that is being generated
Ctrl+Shift+;⌘+Shift+;Copy the last answer
Ctrl+Shift+/⌘+Shift+/Show all shortcuts

Check the Answers

Warning

AI can make mistakes. Before you use an answer in a report or a patient decision, compare key figures, such as counts, dates, and statuses, with the data shown in Workspace.

If an answer doesn't match the page, ask AI Assistant where the figure comes from, or rephrase the question.
