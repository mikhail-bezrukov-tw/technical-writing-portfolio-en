        <h2>Patient Section</h2>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Today</strong> — the landing view: a personalized greeting, an AI-generated insight banner with same-day opportunities, summary counts, the day's schedule, and a HEDIS Composite compliance ring. If a PCP Group is selected instead of an individual provider, Today switches to a Dashboard view aggregating the same metrics across the group.</li>
          <li><strong>Care Gaps</strong> — the patient's open and closed HEDIS/quality gaps, with an AI-generated Patient Summary and links to relevant Treatment Notes.</li>
          <li><strong>Patient View</strong> — a consolidated snapshot of the patient: Care Gaps, Schedule, HEDIS, and Admissions, plus a Patient Summary banner when available.</li>
          <li><strong>HEDIS</strong> — the patient's HEDIS Part C and Part D measures, plus exclusions where applicable.</li>
          <li><strong>Therapeutic Optimizations</strong> — AI-flagged medication opportunities for the patient.</li>
          <li><strong>Voice Notes</strong> — records a voice note for a visit.</li>
          <li><strong>Connect</strong> — the patient's outreach in one place: SMS, calls, messages, and mailed letters.</li>
          <li><strong>Clarifications</strong> — the queue of open documentation clarification tasks.</li>

          <li><strong>Patient Scheduling</strong> — patients with open scheduling-related tasks.</li>
          <li><strong>HEDIS</strong> — practice-wide HEDIS measures and outstanding tasks across the panel.</li>
          <li><strong>Admissions</strong> — recent hospital admissions and discharge events across the panel.</li>
          <li><strong>Connect</strong> — automated outreach tasks with status and outcome tracking.</li>
          <li><strong>Eligibility Check</strong> — real-time insurance coverage verification for prospective patients.</li>
          <li><strong>Clarifications</strong> — the queue of open documentation clarification tasks.</li>
