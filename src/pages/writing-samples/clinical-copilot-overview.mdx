A portfolio-safe version of a production product overview. Product naming and internal Knowledge Base links have been anonymized or removed.

Clinical Copilot is an EMR-embedded plugin that surfaces AI-assisted patient insights, care-gap tracking, and scheduling tools directly inside the clinical workflow.

The plugin is organized into two sections, selectable from the sidebar: **Patient** (patient-centered tools scoped to the currently open record) and **Practice** (panel-wide tools spanning all patients).

## Sidebar

The sidebar is the same in both sections except for the middle group of tabs, which changes when switching between **Patient** and **Practice**.

The top of the sidebar is the same in both sections:

- **Patient / Practice switch** — switches the sidebar between the Patient and Practice sections.
- **Patient Search** — finds a patient to open.
- **AI Assistant** — opens the AI chat.

## Patient Section

- **Today** — the landing view: a personalized greeting, an AI-generated insight banner with same-day opportunities, summary counts, the day's schedule, and a HEDIS Composite compliance ring. If a PCP Group is selected instead of an individual provider, Today switches to a Dashboard view aggregating the same metrics across the group.
- **Care Gaps** — the patient's open and closed HEDIS/quality gaps, with an AI-generated Patient Summary and links to relevant Treatment Notes.
- **Patient View** — a consolidated snapshot of the patient: Care Gaps, Schedule, HEDIS, and Admissions, plus a Patient Summary banner when available.
- **HEDIS** — the patient's HEDIS Part C and Part D measures, plus exclusions where applicable.
- **Therapeutic Optimizations** — AI-flagged medication opportunities for the patient.
- **Voice Notes** — records a voice note for a visit.
- **Connect** — the patient's outreach in one place: SMS, calls, messages, and mailed letters.
- **Clarifications** — the queue of open documentation clarification tasks.

## Practice Section

- **PCPs' Calendar** — a calendar view of appointments across the practice's providers.
- **All Patients** — every patient in the panel, with filtering, sorting, and search.
- **Patient Scheduling** — patients with open scheduling-related tasks.
- **HEDIS** — practice-wide HEDIS measures and outstanding tasks across the panel.
- **Admissions** — recent hospital admissions and discharge events across the panel.
- **Connect** — automated outreach tasks with status and outcome tracking.
- **Eligibility Check** — real-time insurance coverage verification for prospective patients.
- **Clarifications** — the queue of open documentation clarification tasks.

## Bottom of the Sidebar

The bottom of the sidebar contains supporting tools, the user menu, and Settings. Available settings depend on what is enabled for the organization.

## Installation

Clinical Copilot is available in three forms:

- **Browser extension** — runs alongside a supported EMR.
- **Desktop application** — used when working outside an active EMR session.
- **Standalone web application** — runs in the browser without an EMR open.
