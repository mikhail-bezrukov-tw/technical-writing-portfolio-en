    <h1>Today (Patients)</h1>

    <div className="kb-two-column">
      <div className="kb-copy">
        <p>The <strong>Today</strong> page is the central dashboard of Clinical Copilot. It opens by default and gives clinical staff a same-day view of the selected PCP's schedule, along with AI-generated insights and quality metrics.</p>

        <ol className="kb-numbered">
          <li><p>At the top, Clinical Copilot greets the user and shows the date and number of patients scheduled for today. Below the greeting, an AI-generated insight highlights what needs attention — for example, open care gaps that can be closed during today's visits, patients with recent ER admissions, or pending medication refills. <strong>Ask Copilot</strong> opens the AI Assistant with this context already in view.</p></li>
          <li>
            <p><strong>Today's Summary</strong> gives an at-a-glance count of:</p>
            <ul>
              <li><strong>Clarifications</strong> — open documentation clarification tasks.</li>
              <li><strong>Critical Patient(s)</strong> — patients flagged as high-priority.</li>
              <li><strong>Care Gaps</strong> — open care gaps across today's patients.</li>
              <li><strong>HEDIS</strong> — open HEDIS tasks across today's patients.</li>
            </ul>
          </li>
          <li><p>Below the summary, Clinical Copilot lists today's appointments in chronological order, with patient details and visit context. A patient flagged as high-priority shows a <strong>Critical</strong> label. An appointment row opens its details.</p></li>
          <li><p>The <strong>HEDIS Composite</strong> card shows how many HEDIS measures are compliant out of the total tracked for the panel, with the number of measures still needed to reach 100%.</p></li>
        </ol>

        <p>At the bottom of the page, the <strong>Ask anything</strong> bar sends a question directly to the AI Assistant. Suggested prompts provide one-click starting points for common questions.</p>
      </div>

    <h2>Tracking a PCP Group's Performance</h2>
    <p className="kb-location"><strong>Location:</strong> Patients &gt; Today</p>

    <div className="kb-two-column group-grid">
      <div className="kb-copy">
        <p>When a PCP Group is selected instead of a single PCP, Today switches to an aggregated Dashboard view for the whole group. Instead of a single day's schedule, the page shows group performance metrics.</p>

        <p><strong>Summary row</strong> — four scores for the selected group:</p>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Overall Score</strong> — a composite score across key performance metrics.</li>
          <li><strong>Members</strong> — the total number of unique members managed by the group.</li>
          <li><strong>Care Gaps Score</strong> — how well the group manages and closes care gaps.</li>
          <li><strong>Utilization</strong> — how well the group coordinates care and avoids unnecessary utilization.</li>
        </ul>

        <p><strong>Care Gaps Outlook</strong> shows the monthly share of addressed care gaps over the last 12 months.</p>

        <p><strong>Care Gaps Execution</strong> summarizes action and confirmation rates for the group, with targets or period-over-period change where applicable.</p>

        <p><strong>Operational Signals</strong> includes Membership Pulse, HEDIS Composite, and Utilization Follow-up cards for a compact view of panel health.</p>
      </div>
