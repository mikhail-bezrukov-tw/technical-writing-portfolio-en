    <h1>How to Use the AI Assistant in Workspace</h1>

    <p><strong>AI Assistant</strong> is embedded in Workspace. On every Workspace page, it can use the context of the patient, provider, or other record you're viewing, so you can ask questions in plain language.</p>

    <h2>Open AI Assistant</h2>

    <p>The AI Assistant button is the round button at the right edge of the page.</p>

    <figure className="kb-full-shot">
      <img src={useBaseUrl('/img/ai-assistant-open.png')} alt="Workspace page with the AI Assistant button highlighted on the right edge"/>
    </figure>

    <p>To open the chat, do one of the following:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Click the <strong>AI Assistant</strong> button.</li>
      <li>Press <strong>Ctrl+Shift+O</strong> (<strong>⌘+Shift+O</strong> on Mac) to open AI Assistant and start a new chat.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>The AI Assistant chat opens as a compact panel over the current Workspace page.</p>

    <div className="kb-note">
      <strong>Tip</strong>
      <p>If the button covers something you need, drag it to another place along the left or right edge of the screen. Workspace remembers where you leave it.</p>
    </div>

    <h2>If You Don't See AI Assistant</h2>

    <p>The AI Assistant button appears only for accounts with access to the feature. If you don't see the button on any Workspace page, contact your workspace administrator to request access.</p>

    <h2>Ask About Workspace Data</h2>

    <p>Ask AI Assistant about any patient, provider, facility, or other Workspace data from any page, even if you don't have that record open.</p>

    <p>If you already have a patient, provider, or other record open, you don't need to name it in your question. AI Assistant uses what you have open as context, no matter which tab is open. It can also answer questions about something you open over the page, such as a checklist, calendar, task, or document.</p>

    <p>When you open another page while the chat is open, the conversation continues with the data of the new page.</p>

    <p>Some answers appear as cards instead of plain text, for example, a patient list, lab results, care gaps, or appointments.</p>

    <div className="kb-note">
      <strong>Tip</strong>
      <p>Not sure what to ask? Start with “Which of Dr. Smith's patients were admitted to a hospital last month?” or “Summarize Mary Johnson's activity over the last 12 months”, then change the names, dates, or details to fit your work.</p>
    </div>

    <h2>Work with the Chat</h2>

    <p>The AI Assistant chat in Workspace works the same way as in the standalone AI Assistant app.</p>

    <div className="kb-note">
      <strong>Note</strong>
      <p>Chats you start in Workspace are saved to your AI Assistant chat history.</p>
    </div>

    <figure className="kb-full-shot">
      <img src={useBaseUrl('/img/ai-assistant-chat.png')} alt="AI Assistant chat panel with numbered controls over a Workspace data table"/>
    </figure>

    <p>The controls are described below; the numbers match the numbers in the mockup.</p>

| # | Control | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | New chat | Starts a new chat for the page that is open |
| 2 | Chat list | Opens your saved chats. Here you can search, pin, archive, share, or move a chat to a project |
| 3 | Expand | Switches the chat between the compact panel and full screen |
| 4 | Close | Closes the chat panel without deleting the conversation. To return to it, click the AI Assistant button |
| 5 | Paper clip | Attaches a file to your question |
| 6 | Connected services | Lists the services whose data AI Assistant can use. The number next to the label shows how many are selected. Workspace selects the relevant ones for each page automatically |
| 7 | Model menu | Selects the AI model that answers |

    <p>Under each answer, you can copy it, regenerate it, or have it read aloud.</p>

    <h2>Use Keyboard Shortcuts</h2>

    <p><strong>Ctrl+Shift+O</strong> works on any Workspace page. The other shortcuts work while the chat is open.</p>

| Shortcut (Windows) | Shortcut (Mac) | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ctrl+Shift+O** | **⌘+Shift+O** | Start a new chat, even if the chat is hidden |
| **Shift+Esc** | **Shift+Esc** | Move the cursor to the message field |
| **Ctrl+Enter** | **⌘+Enter** | Send the message |
| **Ctrl+Shift+X** | **⌘+Shift+X** | Stop the answer that is being generated |
| **Ctrl+Shift+;** | **⌘+Shift+;** | Copy the last answer |
| **Ctrl+Shift+/** | **⌘+Shift+/** | Show all shortcuts |

    <h2>Check the Answers</h2>

    <div className="kb-warning">
      <strong>Warning</strong>
      <p>AI can make mistakes. Before you use an answer in a report or a patient decision, compare key figures, such as counts, dates, and statuses, with the data shown in Workspace.</p>
    </div>

    <p>If an answer doesn't match the page, ask AI Assistant where the figure comes from, or rephrase the question.</p>
