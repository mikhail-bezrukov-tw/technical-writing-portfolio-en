## Experience

I have nearly four years of experience in software product documentation. In my current role I own end-user and internal documentation for eight healthcare IT services, maintain more than 200 Knowledge Base articles, and contribute to release communication across 18 services.

My day-to-day documentation and stakeholder communication are in English. I am a native Russian speaker, so I am comfortable sourcing technical and product context in Russian and turning it into clear English-language documentation.

## How I work

I treat documentation ownership as more than drafting. My day-to-day work combines product research, source verification, writing, publication and maintenance, Git-based delivery, screenshots and diagrams, and collaboration with Product, Engineering, QA, business analysts, data specialists, and domain experts.

I build reusable Claude and Claude Code skills and AI-assisted workflows that automate repeatable parts of documentation creation and maintenance, including drafting support, editorial QA, UX copy, screenshot preparation, and quality checks. I am particularly interested in keeping the boundary clear between editorial judgment, AI-assisted investigation, and deterministic code-based verification.
